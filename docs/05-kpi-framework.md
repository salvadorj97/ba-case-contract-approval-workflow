# 5. KPI Framework

These are **proposed targets**, set from the pain points and common benchmarks. Before any change, a baseline must be measured for each KPI over at least one month.

## Workflow efficiency

| KPI | Linked to | Proposed target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Average time from review request to signature hand-off | P1 | -20% vs baseline (ideal: within 2 weeks) | Platform analytics |
| Share of reminders sent automatically | P1 | 95% | Platform logs |
| Manual tags in chat groups per week | P1 | Trend to zero | Sample count |
| Average number of review rounds per contract | P2 | Down vs baseline | Platform analytics |

## Quality

| KPI | Linked to | Proposed target | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disagree votes without a usable reason | P2 | Zero | Platform data |
| Contracts rerouted because of a wrong risk level | P3, P4 | Down vs baseline | Contract history |
| Risk overrides with justification | P3 | 100% justified | Platform data |
| High-priority contracts reviewed first | P6 | +25% vs baseline | Platform analytics |

## Adoption and collaboration

| KPI | Proposed target | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Team members trained on the Risk Table and Agile basics | 90% | Training attendance |
| Attendance at sprint ceremonies | 85% | Meeting logs |
| Cross-team satisfaction score | 4.5 / 5 | Post-sprint survey |

## Review rhythm

- **Every sprint (2 weeks):** workflow and quality KPIs, blockers.
- **Every quarter:** adoption, satisfaction, and whether the targets themselves are still right.

## Risks after go-live

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Teams return to chat-group tagging | Keep the history visible, retrospectives |
| Uneven adoption across countries | Pilot in two countries first, then roll out |
| Risk Table criteria misused | Mandatory justification on overrides, refresher training |
